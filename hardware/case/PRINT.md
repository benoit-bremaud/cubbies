# Printing the cubbies case (fablab sheet)

One page to carry to the fablab. Everything is in `export/print/`, generated by
`cubbies_case.py`: each part is already turned the right way and resting on the bed, so
**import and slice without rotating anything**.

## Files

| File | Content | Approx. size (x × y × height) |
|------|---------|------|
| `coupon-plate.3mf` | **print first**: the tolerance coupon (2 pieces) + one real rear cover | small, ~20 min (est.) |
| `cubbies-plate.3mf` | one cube: front shell, rear shell, rear cover | ~2 h (est.) |
| `coupon-front.stl`, `coupon-rear.stl` | coupon pieces, if the slicer prefers STL | 6 × 12 × 12, 35 × 30 × 33 mm |
| `cubbies-front.stl` | front shell, front face on the bed | 40 × 40 × 12 mm |
| `cubbies-rear.stl` | rear shell, back face on the bed (hooks up) | 40 × 40 × 33 mm |
| `cubbies-rear-cover.stl` | module bay cover, outer face on the bed (hooks up) | 25 × 24 × 7 mm |

The 3MF files hold geometry only (one object per part, already placed), so every slicer opens
them: PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, Cura. The fablab's printer and slicer are not
known yet; once they are, a slicer project with the settings below can be saved from the
plate and committed next to it. Times are estimates.

## Settings (`docs/hardware/mechanical-spec.md`)

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Material | PLA, the cube's colour or neutral white |
| Nozzle | 0.4 mm |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Perimeters / walls | 4 (the 1.6 mm walls are then solid perimeters) |
| Top / bottom layers | 5 / 4 |
| Infill | 20 % (only the thick spots: rails, hook roots) |
| Supports | **none** (overhangs ≤ 45°, bridges ≤ 20 mm) |
| Brim | none; add a 3 mm brim to the rear shell only if the bed adhesion is poor |
| Scale | 100 %, no XY compensation (the 0.2 mm clearance is in the model) |

## Order and checks

1. **Coupon.** Push the hook piece into the pocket piece: it clicks, holds when shaken, and
   comes apart with a firm pull without breaking. Drop the cover into the frame: it seats
   flush, clips, and lifts out with a fingernail in the notch.
   - Too tight (won't go in, or a hook cracks): try an "XY hole compensation" of
     +0.05 mm in the slicer, or note it and we raise `TOL` in the script.
   - Too loose (no click, falls out): note it; we lower `TOL` or raise the barb.
2. **One cube.** Print `cubbies-plate.3mf`, assemble (the shells snap together, the cover
   clips in the back) and check the 40 mm cube closes without a gap at the split.
3. **The series**, one cube per colleague, in each colour, only after 1 and 2 pass.

Write the results (printer, slicer, settings actually used, fit of each check, photos) in
`docs/hardware/assembly.md`: they are the mechanical CDR print-and-fit evidence.
